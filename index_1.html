01 / About

Where the work sits

Growing up in Naharlagun, a small town in Arunachal Pradesh, I didn't have the typical CS kid story. No coding bootcamps at twelve, no startup garage. What I had was curiosity — about how things work, why people make the choices they do, and what happens when you mix the right spices in the wrong order.

That curiosity led me to IISER Bhopal, and eventually to a fairly specific corner of machine learning: deep generative models and representation learning. What a model learns is rarely the thing you asked it to learn, and almost never in a form that lines up with another model's. Most of my work is some version of that problem.

At National Chung Cheng University in Taiwan I treated fusing two heterogeneous Vision Transformers as a graph alignment problem and solved it with optimal transport. In an ongoing project I'm using a gradient reversal layer to strip demographic signal out of speech representations so a Parkinson's classifier stops leaning on the speaker instead of the disease. In another, I'm training generative models on the handloom motifs of Arunachal Pradesh — a domain with almost no data and a great deal at stake in getting it right. Earlier work at IIT Mandi and IIT Bhubaneswar covered optimization for drone patrols, spatiotemporal graphs for trajectory prediction, and LLMs for geospatial reasoning.

Around that core, there's a set of things I keep reading about and want to work on properly: human–AI interaction, privacy-preserving learning, and game-theoretic views of multi-agent systems. And when I'm not on any of it, you'll find me cooking a recipe from my never-ending list, writing nostalgic threads of memory on Medium, or disappearing into a travel magazine.

02 / Experience

Labs, and everything else

National Chung Cheng University, Taiwan · Research Intern
Multi-Scale Optimal Transport for Heterogeneous Transformer Fusion
Jun 2026 – Aug 2026 · TEEP, Chiayi, Taiwan
Dept. of Computer Science and Information Engineering. I developed a multi-scale Optimal Transport framework that treats fusing heterogeneous transformers as a computational graph alignment problem — a macro-to-micro pipeline combining Fused Gromov–Wasserstein transport, Wasserstein barycentric synthesis, and Sinkhorn-based parameter alignment. Rather than padding 6-layer and 12-layer Vision Transformers to a shared identity, the barycentric step resolves the architectural depth mismatch directly. On CIFAR-100 the fused model reached 49.74% test accuracy after 15 fine-tuning epochs, outperforming both independently trained parents.
Optimal Transport Fused Gromov–Wasserstein Sinkhorn Vision Transformers Model Fusion
IIT Mandi · Summer Research Intern
Drone Surveillance & Trajectory Prediction
Jun 2025 – Jul 2025 · Mandi, HP
Under Dr. Dinesh Singh, with mentorship from Tarun Saini (Ph.D.), I worked on two problems at the intersection of optimization, robotics and machine learning. First: scheduling drone patrols to minimise flight time under patrol-interval constraints — ILP, Greedy, Simulated Annealing, and a novel Multi-Stage method, tested on real campus maps (Ritsumeikan University, IISER Bhopal) for high coverage at reduced runtime. What I've been building

01 Ongoing

Deep Generative Systems for Indigenous Textile Archiving

An independent project applying deep learning to digitise, classify and re-envision the indigenous handloom motifs of Arunachal Pradesh. CNN/GAN pipelines for regional motif classification and generative synthesis, a searchable dataset integrating visual data, metadata and cultural narratives, and a prototype AI-assisted pattern-design toolkit — with neural style transfer fusing traditional motifs into contemporary aesthetics.

CNN · GAN · Style Transfer · Jan 2026 –
02 Ongoing

Demographic-Agnostic Parkinson's Detection

A fairness-aware deep learning framework detecting Parkinson's from Mel-spectrogram representations of speech. A tri-branch adversarial CNN with a Gradient Reversal Layer disentangles pathological features from demographic attributes, over an audio pipeline using VAD, STFT, Mel-scale transformation and SpecAugment. Cross-linguistic generalisation is evaluated across mPower, PC-GITA and Czech PD.

PyTorch · Adversarial Learning · Speech · Apr 2026 –
03

Multi-Scale Optimal Transport Transformer Fusion

Fusing heterogeneous Vision Transformers by formulating model fusion as computational graph alignment. Fused Gromov–Wasserstein transport handles the macro structure, Wasserstein barycentric synthesis resolves the 6-vs-12-layer depth mismatch without heuristic identity padding, and Sinkhorn alignment handles parameters. 49.74% on CIFAR-100 after 15 fine-tuning epochs — past both parent models.

Optimal Transport · FGW · Sinkhorn

Multi-Stage Drone Surveillance Scheduling

A novel Multi-Stage algorithmic framework for minimising drone flight time under patrol-interval constraints, benchmarked against ILP, Greedy and Simulated Annealing. Tested on real university campus maps, achieving high area coverage with significantly lower runtime than traditional approaches.

Trajectron: Multi-Agent Trajectory Prediction

A graph-based deep generative model using Conditional VAEs and dynamic spatiotemporal graphs for predicting pedestrian trajectories. Evaluated on ETH/UCY benchmarks — competitive ADE, FDE and NLL, outperforming baselines such as Social GAN.
